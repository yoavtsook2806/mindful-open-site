Mindful Open – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Mindful Open is an Android app that shows a short countdown before the apps you choose open. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Data we collect

None. Mindful Open does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no network communication of its own.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (the list of apps you chose to delay and the countdown length) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They may be included in your device's regular Android backup if you have backups enabled; that backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by us. Uninstalling the app deletes these settings.

Accessibility service

Mindful Open uses the Android AccessibilityService API for one purpose: to notice when one of the apps you selected comes to the foreground so it can show the countdown overlay. The service is configured so that it cannot read the content of your screen; it only receives the name of the app whose window just appeared. Nothing it observes is recorded or transmitted. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

Installed apps

To let you pick which apps to delay, the app lists the apps installed on your device (the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission). This list is shown to you inside the app only and is never stored elsewhere or sent off the device.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
